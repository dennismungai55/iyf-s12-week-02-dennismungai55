Understanding the CSS Box Model

1. Content

Content is the actual text or image inside an element. Its background color helps identify the content area.

2. Padding

Padding is the space between the content and the border. It increases the internal space of an element.

3. Border

Border is the visible line surrounding the padding and content.

4. Margin

Margin is the space outside the border that separates one element from another.
